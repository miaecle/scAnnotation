Description: Airway epithelial cell with motile cilia expressing FOXJ1 and multiple dynein genes; often misannotated as goblet when appearing as a columnar cell with apical structures.

Genes:
- FOXJ1, CFAP53, CFAP221, CFAP54, DNAH5, DNAH9, DNAH11, DNAH12, DNAI1, DNAI2, DNALI1, DRC1
- CCDC39, CCDC40, CFAP300, SPAG1, LRRC6, ZMYND10, C21orf59, GAS8, TUBB4B, TUBB4A, RP2, RUVBL1
- CFAP157

### Club cell

Description: Non-ciliated bronchiolar secretory cell that secretes club cell secretory protein (CC16/SCGB1A1) and detoxifies xenobiotics; often confused with goblet cells due to similar secretory morphology.

Genes:
- SCGB1A1, SCGB3A2, CYP2F1, CYP2A13, CYP2B6, CYP4B1, EPHX1, ALDH3A1, CES1, SULT2B1, LDHB, PIGR
- BPIFA1, BPIFB1, LCN2, SAA1, SAA2, CHCHD10, MT2A, GSTM3, GSTA1, TIPARP, ORM1, ORM2
- FABP5

### MUC5AC-high goblet cell

Description: Airway surface goblet cell subtype characterized by high expression of MUC5AC, involved in mucin production and mucosal defense; commonly found in proximal lung airways.

Genes:
- MUC5AC, SPDEF, FOXA3, AGR2, TFF3, CLCA1, ZG16B, FCGBP, CXCL17, CEACAM5, CEACAM6, MUC13
- ERN2, XBP1, HES6, TFCP2L1, B3GNT6, GALNT12, GALNT7, ST6GALNAC1, SLC5A8, NFE2L3, KLK11, KLK13
- TCN1, HP, LCN2, SAA1, BPIFB1, DMBT1

### MUC5B-high goblet cell

Description: Submucosal gland mucous cell subtype preferentially expressing MUC5B, contributing to mucus rheology and found in submucosal glands of the tracheobronchial tree.

Genes:
- MUC5B, SPDEF, FOXA3, AGR2, TFF3, CLCA1, ZG16B, FCGBP, CXCL17, CEACAM6, MUC13, ERN2
- XBP1, HES6, TFCP2L1, GALNT12, GALNT7, ST6GALNAC1, SLC5A8, NFE2L3, KLK13, TCN1, HP, LCN2
- BPIFB1, BPIFA2, LYZ, PLA2G2A, MUC7, PIGR
