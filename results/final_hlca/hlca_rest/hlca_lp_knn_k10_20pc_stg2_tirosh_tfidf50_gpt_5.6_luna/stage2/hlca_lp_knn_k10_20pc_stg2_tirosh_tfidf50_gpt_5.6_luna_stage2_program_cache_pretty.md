## vein endothelial cell

### ACKR1-positive systemic venous and postcapillary venular endothelial cells

Description: Venular endothelial cells specialized for leukocyte recruitment and blood-tissue exchange, often marked by ACKR1, adhesion molecules, and COL15A1.

Genes:
- ACKR1, SELP, VCAM1, SELE, ICAM1, NR2F2, COL15A1, PLVAP, EMCN, VWF, PECAM1, CDH5
- ENG, RAMP2, KDR, ADGRL4, IL33, CXCL14, EDNRB, CA4

### Arterial endothelial cells

Description: Arterial endothelial cells are specialized for high-pressure blood flow and are distinguished by EFNB2, SOX17, GJA5, DLL4, and SEMA3G.

Genes:
- EFNB2, SOX17, GJA5, FBLN5, SEMA3G, HEY1, DLL4, BMX, MECOM, CXCL12, BCL6B, FZD4
- GJA4, EPAS1, PECAM1, CDH5, KDR, VWF, ENG, RAMP2

### General and aerocyte pulmonary capillary endothelial cells

Description: Pulmonary capillary endothelial cells form the gas-exchange interface and include CA4-positive aerocytes and GPIHBP1-positive general capillary cells.

Genes:
- CA4, EDNRB, EMCN, GPIHBP1, RGCC, BTNL9, FABP4, SOSTDC1, FCN3, EDN1, APLNR, ADGRL4
- PECAM1, CDH5, KDR, VWF, PLVAP, RAMP2, EPAS1, KLF2

### Lymphatic endothelial cells

Description: Lymphatic endothelial cells drain interstitial fluid and immune cells and are defined by PDPN, LYVE1, CCL21, FLT4, and PROX1.

Genes:
- PDPN, LYVE1, CCL21, FLT4, PROX1, FOXC2, NRP2, CCL14, GJC2, TBX1, RELN, ACKR4
- EFNB2, FABP4, CLDN5, EMCN, PECAM1, CDH5, KDR, RAMP2

### Pulmonary venous endothelial cells

Description: Venous endothelial cells lining pulmonary veins and larger venular channels, with a venous transcriptional program and enhanced barrier and trafficking functions.

Genes:
- NR2F2, VWF, SELP, VCAM1, PLVAP, COL15A1, RGCC, EMCN, PECAM1, CDH5, ENG, RAMP2
- KDR, ADGRL4, EPAS1, KLF2, KLF4, EDNRB, CA4, CXCL14
